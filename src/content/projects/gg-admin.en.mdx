## Why it exists

Firestore is great for transactional writes (high throughput, realtime listeners), but
poor for analytical queries at scale. As GG Checkout grew, reports became slow —
aggregations over millions of transactions in Firestore don't scale.

The solution was to split transactional and analytical concerns: Firestore remains the
source of truth, and a sync pipeline replicates data to ClickHouse, which is optimized
for OLAP queries. The dashboard queries ClickHouse for all reports.

## Outcome

Dashboards that used to take seconds to load now respond in milliseconds. ClickHouse also
unlocks richer analyses (funnels, cohorts, time series) that would be unfeasible on
Firestore.
